      04 · Easy size guide

      💚 Is my picture too big?

      ✓ 500 KB or less: Good target for one sticker preview.

      ⚠ Over 500 KB: Consider resizing or exporting as WebP.

      ⚠ Over 1 MB: Worth optimizing before publishing.

      Try around 1200 × 1200 px for previews. Keep high-resolution print masters backed up elsewhere.

      These are suggestions for loading performance, not GitHub storage limits. The meter excludes unreferenced files, portfolio artwork, and other site assets.
